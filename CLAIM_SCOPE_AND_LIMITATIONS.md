# Claim scope

The input allows arbitrarily many candidates and voters. Each full bundle contains all potential voters within the fixed inclusive distance, including its leader. There are at most three voters per bundle. The number k of leaders is an input budget. Ties count for the main co-winner theorem. The related unique-winner variant adds one registered p vote.

The structural lift applies at fixed radii at least two. The work leaves radius one with bundles of size three, parameterized hardness in k, and restricted candidate domains outside the claim. Earlier work covers the complexity at larger radii without the specific five-path restriction. The bounded literature review makes no absolute priority claim.
