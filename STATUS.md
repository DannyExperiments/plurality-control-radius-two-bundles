# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/plurality-control-radius-two-bundles/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24570446)

Deciding whether adding the bundles of at most k leaders makes a candidate a plurality co-winner is NP-complete when each bundle contains exactly the potential voters within two adjacent swaps of its leader and has at most three voters. The hardness holds when the distance graph is a disjoint union of induced five-vertex paths.

The internal mathematical review covered the main proof and the two variants. External peer review and formal verification have not been conducted. See the [mathematical review](audits/public_safe_reports/MATHEMATICS.md) and [source comparison](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE.md).
