Why these choices work

A = (x₀, y₀, z₀), B = (x₀, y₁, z₁), C = (x₁, y₁, z₁). The initial score of p is six and the fixed rival g has nine. Voters 1 and 5 cannot add the middle p-voter. Leaders 2, 3, and 4 are choices L, M, and R.

L adds y and z, M adds z and x, and R adds two x votes besides the vote for p. In the default selection L, R, L, only A and C use L, so they cover each x, y, and z once. Change A from L to M to see x₀ exceed the cap. Change only B from R to L to see y₁ and z₁ exceed it.
